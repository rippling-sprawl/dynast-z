Readme

Rankings are private to your account and saved per league. Columns: Player (required), Pos, Team, Tier, MyRank, Grade, Target. Grade accepts like or fade — older sheets' love and avoid are read as those. You can also set it on the board itself, and it weights the Targets projection. Target is your Targets queue: mark a row Y to queue him; importing applies it only to the players in the file, so anyone else's queue entry is left alone. Any extra columns you keep are preserved.
An import merges into the rankings you already have — you pick which columns it applies, players it doesn't mention are left untouched, and nothing it can do will remove a player from your board.
