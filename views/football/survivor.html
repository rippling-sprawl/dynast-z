How it works

  • Each week you pick one team to win its game outright. No spread — a three-point win and a thirty-point win are the same thing here. The line is printed on the board anyway, greyed, because it is still the best one-number answer to who is likely to win.
  • Your team wins, you survive to next week. Your team loses and you are out — for the season, not for the week.
  • A tie counts as a loss. It is the default the big contests run, and it is the only reading under which "survive" means what it says.
  • A team can only be picked once all season. Spending Kansas City in week 2 is the real cost of a week 2 pick. Teams you have already used are greyed out on the board and cannot be picked again.
  • Miss a week and you are out. Once every game of a week has kicked off there is no pick left to make, and an entry with nothing down that week is done.
  • Your pick locks when the game you picked kicks off, and not before — so you can change your mind all week, including after Thursday night, as long as your game has not started.
  • Nobody can see your pick until its game kicks off. After that, everyone can, on the standings.
  • There is one entry per account, no buybacks and no second chances. Whoever is still standing after week 18 splits it; if everybody goes out in the same week, they split it between them.
