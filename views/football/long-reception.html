              Method

              1. Where it comes from. nflverse play-by-play for 2024–2026, reduced to every completed reception with its receiver, yardage and defense. The prices are a DraftKings capture: this market is in no odds API — balldontlie carries 18 NFL prop types and this is not one of them.
              2. Per-catch explosiveness. Pooled catch-yardage survival S(x) is measured, not assumed — its tail is heavier than exponential. Each receiver gets one exponent, S_player(x) = S(x)^α, estimated as α = n / −Σ ln S(y) from every catch rather than the rare long ones. That is what makes a 40+ number estimable when the median pass-catcher has two 40+ catches in three seasons. BPI is 1/α.
              3. Volume and defense. λ(x) = catches/game × S(x)^α × defense, player terms shrunk toward position means, defense position-specific. P(longest ≥ x) = 1 − e^−λ(x), with one global λ scale fit on a held-out season.
              4. Edge. likelihood − 1/decimal odds against that exact line; EV = (likelihood × decimal) − 1.

              The sample gate is not decoration: ungated, the biggest edge on the board belongs to a receiver with zero career catches, where the model reports a position average rather than evidence. Those players are tagged THIN. The model also knows nothing about injuries, depth-chart changes or coverage, and the book does — where they disagree about a receiver's volume this week, the book is probably right. The durable claim is the shape of a receiver's yardage plus a measured matchup.
