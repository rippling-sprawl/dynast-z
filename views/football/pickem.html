How it works

  • Pick a side in every game of the week.
  • Your best call is worth the most. Give each pick a confidence from 1 to N, where N is the number of games in the week, using each number once. A correct pick scores its confidence — and a wrong one loses it. Rank a game high only if you mean it.
  • Picks are graded against the spread, not on who wins outright.
  • The line is the consensus of the major sportsbooks as it stood at 3:00 AM ET on Tuesday, and it never moves after that. Everyone is graded against the same number.
  • A push — the favorite wins by exactly the spread — costs nothing and pays nothing, for anyone.
  • Each game locks at its own kickoff. Until then nobody can see your pick; after it, everyone can.
